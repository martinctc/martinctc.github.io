What I do

I am a Senior Data Scientist at Microsoft working on Viva Insights and Copilot Analytics. My role combines hands-on analytics development with customer-facing advisory work, helping large organizations understand how work gets done and how AI is changing workplace behavior.

A lot of my recent work focuses on:

  • Employee wellbeing and collaboration effectiveness
  • AI adoption and Copilot ROI analysis
  • Applied analytics for enterprise decision-making
  • Reproducible data science workflows in R and Python

I also author and maintain open-source tools for practitioners working with workplace analytics data:

  • {vivainsights} (R) (CRAN)
  • vivainsights (Python) (PyPI)
  • Viva Insights Sample Code

Before Microsoft, I worked in strategy consulting at Rainmakers CSI, where I supported clients on open-ended strategic questions using a mix of quantitative and qualitative research. That background still shapes how I approach analytics today: hypothesis-driven, practical, and decision-focused.

Check out my LinkedIn to find out more.

How I got into R

Before I became a heavy R user, I learned Excel VBA as a "first language" and quickly saw how much better work gets when repetitive processes are automated well. I later moved into R for its analytical depth, open-source ecosystem, and strong community norms around reproducibility and collaboration.
